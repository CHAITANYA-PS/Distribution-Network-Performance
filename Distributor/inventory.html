Request Status

Manufacturer-controlled inventory exchange progress.

  1. Request SubmittedThe shortage request was sent to the manufacturer.
  2. Manufacturer ReviewThe requested product, quantity and shortage reason are being checked.
  3. Supplier Assignment / TransitAn approved distributor is assigned and the stock movement is tracked.
  4. Receipt VerificationThe requesting distributor confirms the received quantity and condition.
  5. CompletedThe inventory exchange request is closed.
